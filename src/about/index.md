I’m Shrinath Prabhu, a Senior Staff Frontend Engineer based in Mumbai, India. I have more than eight years of professional experience building web applications, browser extensions, developer tools, and Web3 products. This website is my personal portfolio and blog.

## What I work on

At Avail Project, I lead frontend architecture across the Nexus product ecosystem. My work includes Avail Widgets, a set of React components for cross-chain transactions; the Widget Configurator; and Nexus Fastbridge. I care about the complete experience: how a product is structured, how it behaves when something goes wrong, and how people get from their first visit to a useful result.

Before Avail, I led frontend engineering at Arcana Network for four and a half years, through its acquisition by Avail in 2025. Earlier, at Hashbinary Services, I worked on browser extensions, analytics dashboards, and mobile applications. The [work page](/work/) explains the projects and outcomes, and my [resume](/resume/) covers my experience and skills.

## How I work

I enjoy zero-to-one products, problems that have not yet been clearly defined, and flows where reliability matters. My approach is to scope carefully, make decisions in writing, ship early, and stay accountable after launch. My [blog](/blog/) shares lessons and opinions about engineering, AI, security, product development, and technical leadership.
